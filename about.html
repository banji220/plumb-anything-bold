About the crew

Plumbers who pick up the phone.

Plumb Anything is a family-owned crew of licensed plumbers serving all of Orange County since 2009. No call centers, no upsells — just the same faces, the same trucks, and the same two-year guarantee on every job we finish.

What makes us different

Most plumbing companies operate as booking desks that dispatch subcontractors. We're the opposite: a small crew of full-time W-2 plumbers, dispatched by the owner, driving trucks we own. When you call, you get a real technician on the line inside of two rings.

Fixed pricing, always

Before we lift a wrench you'll see a written, itemized quote. If the job takes longer, that's on us — the price you approve is the price you pay.

Two-year workmanship guarantee

Every repair and install is guaranteed for 24 months, parts and labor. If it fails, we come back — no invoice, no arguments.
