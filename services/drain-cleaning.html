Our drain services

Standard cabling —

For kitchen, bath, and laundry branch lines up to 3". Removes hair, grease, and soap buildup in one pass.

Hydro jetting

4,000 PSI water scours the pipe wall clean. The right call for repeat clogs, root intrusion, and grease-heavy commercial kitchens.

Camera inspection — included on mains

Color video down the line means we can show you exactly why the drain backed up — and confirm the fix before we leave.

When to call us instead of a hardware-store snake

If the same drain keeps clogging, if you smell sewage, if multiple fixtures back up at once, or if you see water at the cleanout — that's a mainline job that needs professional cabling or jetting.
