Plumbing that fits San Juan Capistrano’s homes

San Juan is one of the oldest settlements in California, and its historic core near the Mission and Los Rios Street holds some of the oldest homes in the county — adobe-era and early-1900s structures with plumbing that reflects their age. The Village and downtown neighborhoods mix these historic homes with mid-century infill.

Beyond the core, the city spreads into rolling, semi-rural terrain: equestrian neighborhoods with larger lots, hillside communities like Pacifica San Juan and the areas off Ortega Highway, and newer developments with copper, PEX, and tankless systems. The larger lots bring long private water and sewer runs, and some outlying properties have their own layout quirks.

That span — from historic cottages to modern custom homes on acreage — keeps our San Juan work varied. Whatever the property, we quote a flat rate in writing and plan the job around what the home actually is.

What we fix most in San Juan Capistrano

Our common calls range from galvanized repipes and cast-iron drain work in the historic core to slab leaks in mid-century homes and leak detection across the long private runs on the larger equestrian lots. We camera and locate before recommending anything.

In the newer hillside communities we handle tankless service, pressure-regulator work, and fixture replacement. Hard water keeps water-heater work steady, and the bigger properties keep our leak-detection skills busy.

Neighborhoods we serve in San Juan Capistrano

The Mission and Los Rios historic district, downtown Village, the equestrian neighborhoods, Pacifica San Juan, and the areas off Ortega Highway. We cover the 92675 ZIP code.
