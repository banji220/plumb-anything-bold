Plumbing that fits Irvine’s homes

Irvine is the county’s master-planned showcase, built village by village from the 1970s onward. The older villages — Woodbridge, University Park, Turtle Rock, El Camino Real — are 1970s–1980s homes, many with copper supply and slab construction that’s now old enough to develop the occasional slab leak. Woodbridge’s lakes and mature landscaping also bring established trees and the drain issues that follow.

The newer villages — Northwood, Quail Hill, Woodbury, Portola Springs, Great Park, and Cypress Village — are 1990s–2010s and beyond, built with PEX supply, tankless water heaters, pressure-regulating valves, and recirculation systems. These homes rarely fail from corrosion; instead they need service on tankless units, PRVs, angle stops, and fixtures, plus the occasional builder-era defect.

Knowing which Irvine village you’re in tells us a lot before we arrive. Whether it’s a Woodbridge slab leak or a Woodbury tankless service, we quote a flat rate up front and work clean in homes that expect it.

What we fix most in Irvine

In the older villages, our common calls are slab leaks, aging water heaters, and drain stoppages under mature landscaping. We locate slab leaks electronically before cutting so the fix is targeted and the price is known.

In the newer villages we do more tankless service and replacement, pressure-regulator swaps, angle-stop and fixture work, and leak detection on multi-story runs. Irvine’s hard water still scales tankless heat exchangers, so descaling and maintenance are a regular ask.

Irvine plumbing FAQ

Common questions from Irvine homeowners

My newer Irvine home has a tankless water heater that’s acting up. Can you help?

Yes — tankless service is routine for us across Irvine’s newer villages. Hard water scales the heat exchanger over time, and we descale, repair, or replace with a flat price up front.

Do older Irvine homes get slab leaks?

They can. Villages like Woodbridge and University Park date to the 1970s–80s with copper under the slab, which occasionally develops leaks. We locate them electronically and fix them without tearing up the whole floor.

Can you install a water softener or address hard water?

We can advise on it. Irvine’s hard water scales fixtures and tankless units, and we’ll walk you through options that actually make sense for your home and give you a fixed price.
