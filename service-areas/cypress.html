Plumbers in Cypress, CA

Cypress is a tidy west-county city built almost entirely of 1960s and 1970s tract homes — consistent slab construction whose original pipes are exactly the work we do most.

Plumbing that fits Cypress’s homes

Cypress filled in during the 1960s and 1970s, giving it an unusually uniform housing stock: single-story slab-on-grade tract homes with copper supply under the slab and cast-iron or early ABS drains. That consistency means the whole city’s plumbing is aging on roughly the same schedule.

Sitting in the flat west end of the county near the Los Alamitos border, Cypress picks up a touch of marine influence but behaves mostly like a classic postwar suburb. Neighborhoods around Lincoln Avenue, Ball Road, and the Sorrento area all share the same slab-era construction.

With so many near-identical homes, we diagnose Cypress problems quickly and quote a flat rate up front. When we’ve worked the same slab layout countless times, there’s no guesswork slowing the job down.

What we fix most in Cypress

Slab leaks are the signature Cypress call, followed by aging water heaters and mainline stoppages in homes still on original cast-iron. We locate slab leaks electronically before cutting and give you an honest repair-versus-repipe choice.

Hard water keeps water-heater replacement steady, and camera inspection tells us whether a recurring backup is a simple clearing or a failing section. Flat price first, then the fix.

Neighborhoods we serve in Cypress

Common questions from Cypress homeowners

Why are slab leaks common in Cypress?

Most of the city was built in the 1960s and 70s with copper under the slab. After fifty-plus years, that copper starts to leak, making slab leaks one of our most frequent Cypress calls. We locate and price them precisely.

Can you replace my old water heater the same visit?

Often, yes. We carry common sizes, size the unit correctly for your Cypress home, and quote the flat installed price before we start.

Do you offer whole-house repiping in Cypress?

We do — it’s one of our flagship services. If your home is leaking in more than one place, a repipe often beats repeated repairs, and we’ll show you both prices.
