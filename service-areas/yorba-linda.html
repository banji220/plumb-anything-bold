Plumbing that fits Yorba Linda’s homes

Yorba Linda ranges widely in age. The older, established neighborhoods near the historic center and along Yorba Linda Boulevard include 1960s–1970s homes on copper-under-slab construction now old enough to develop leaks. These are the areas where slab leaks and water-heater replacements are most common.

East and north, the city climbs into newer hillside estates — East Lake, Vista del Verde, and the communities toward the 241 — built from the 1990s through the 2010s with copper, PEX, tankless systems, and pressure-regulating valves. Yorba Linda’s larger lots also mean longer private water and drain runs and more landscape irrigation near the house.

That spread keeps our work varied: slab leaks and repipes in the established tracts, tankless and pressure service in the hillside estates, and leak detection across the bigger properties. Every job is quoted flat and in writing before we start.
