Plumbers in Newport Beach, CA

From the tight lots of Balboa Island to the bluff-top estates of Corona del Mar, Newport Beach plumbing means salt air, tricky access, and high standards — all of which we’re built for.

Plumbing that fits Newport Beach’s homes

Newport is really several plumbing worlds at once. On the Peninsula and Balboa Island, homes sit on tiny lots with almost no setback, many rebuilt tall and narrow — access is tight, and the constant marine air corrodes fittings and water heaters quickly. Older cottages that survived here often still hide galvanized or early copper worth replacing.

Up the hill, Newport Coast, Corona del Mar, and the bluffs above the harbor are largely 1980s–2000s custom estates with copper, PEX, tankless systems, recirculation pumps, and multi-story runs. These homes expect a high level of finish, and the plumbing behind the walls has to match — done right, quietly, and cleanly.

Inland, around Newport Heights and the areas near Fashion Island and the Back Bay, you get a mix of mid-century and later homes. Across all of it, the coast rules: we favor corrosion-resistant materials, plan for difficult access, and give a flat-rate price before we begin.

What we fix most in Newport Beach

Our common Newport calls are salt-air corrosion on fittings and water heaters, slab and wall leaks in the older Peninsula and Heights homes, and recirculation, pressure, and tankless service in the custom estates. We locate leaks precisely because opening a wall in a high-finish Newport home is not something to do twice.

On the island and Peninsula, tight access and older cast-iron drains keep our drain and sewer work steady. We choose materials and methods suited to a home right on the water, not whatever is cheapest to throw in.
